From the river to your lorry

Everything happens at one site in FELCRA Sungai Malau.

  1. Dredged

    Our dredge pulls sand from the bed of Sungai Ijok.

  2. Washed

    It is pumped into settling ponds, where clay and silt wash out.

  3. Stockpiled

    Clean sand dries on the pile, ready for loading.

  4. Weighed

    Every lorry crosses our weighbridge on the way out.
